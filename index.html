BANPICK RULES

BO1

  1. Team A bans 1 map
  2. Team B bans 2 maps
  3. Team A bans 2 maps
  4. Team B bans 1 map
  5. Remaining map is played (Knife for sides)

BO3

  1. Team A bans 1 map
  2. Team B bans 1 map
  3. Team A picks a map
  4. Team B picks side (CT/T) on Team A's pick
  5. Team B picks a map
  6. Team A picks side (CT/T) on Team B's pick
  7. Team A bans 1 map
  8. Team B bans 1 map
  9. Remaining map = Decider (Knife)

PREMIER

  1. Team A bans 2 maps
  2. Team B bans 3 maps
  3. Team A bans 1 of the last 2 maps (picks the map)
  4. Remaining map is played
  5. Team B picks starting side (CT/T)
